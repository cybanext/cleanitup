Using Cleansit the right way

Cleansit kills 99.9% of germs and keeps your toilet seat safe and hygienic.

Directions

  1. Spray evenly across the toilet seat surface.
  2. Leave it for a few seconds.
  3. Wipe dry with tissue — then sit with confidence.

Precautions

  1. Avoid contact with your eyes. If it happens, rinse quickly with water.
  2. Keep out of reach of children.
  3. Not a skin product — for seat surfaces only.
  4. Store in a cool, dry place below 30°C. Flammable: keep away from flames.
